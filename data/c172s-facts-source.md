## Not from the POH (wording only, no numbers)
- "ROLL", "PITCH", "YAW", "OPPOSITE DEFLECTION": standard flight-control terms; this POH does not use them.
- Aileron / elevator / trim-tab / rudder travel in the animation is illustrative (POH gives no travel limits); no degree values are shown for them.
- Engine geometry (cylinder, sump, intake and exhaust shapes/placement) and the fuel-tank outline extent are schematic.

## Left out (not verifiable in this POH)
- Flaps "add lift and drag" — the POH describes the flap system but not its aerodynamic effect.
- G1000 / glass panel — this abridged POH describes the conventional panel; no G1000.
- 28-volt electrical system (POH 7-29: 28 V DC, 60 A alternator, 24 V battery) is verifiable but cut for time.
